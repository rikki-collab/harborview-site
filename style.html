03 — Imagery

Harbor light, real buildings.

Atmosphere comes from the water: cool, slightly desaturated harbor photography behind the big statements. The deals use Harborview's own photos of the actual properties, left honest rather than polished.

Treatment on all site photography: saturation pulled to about 75%, slight contrast lift, navy gradient where text sits on top. White sections carry a faint paper grain so they never read as flat screen-white.
